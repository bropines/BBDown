# Community Code of Conduct

This project is committed to providing a friendly, inclusive, and harassment-free collaborative environment.

## Our Commitment

As maintainers and contributors of this project, we commit to making participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address)
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, discussion groups, PR reviews, issue trackers, and any public or private communication related to the project.

## Contact

Report violations or inappropriate behavior to project maintainers via the public channels on their GitHub profiles.
